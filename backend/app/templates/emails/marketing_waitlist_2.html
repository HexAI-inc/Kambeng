Successful campaigns break their target into milestones.

Instead of asking for 50,000 GMD for "school renovation", they ask for 15,000 for cement, then 20,000 for roofing, then 15,000 for paint. Donors can see exactly what their money achieves at each stage — and they come back to fund the next milestone.

When you set up your campaign, take five extra minutes to add 2–4 milestones. It's the highest-impact thing you can do.
