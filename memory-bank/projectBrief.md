## Project Summary

Mobile-first crowdfunding platform for the Gambian market with Wave money donation flows, campaign discovery, admin moderation, and expenditure proof uploads.



## Goals

- Build a trustworthy, mobile-first crowdfunding experience
- Optimize donation and campaign browsing for low-end smartphones
- Support clear admin and moderation workflows



## Constraints

- Primary action color is Wave Blue (#1dc5ff)
- UI must stay legible on smaller screens and low-bandwidth devices
- Use rounded, accessible components with high-contrast CTAs



## Stakeholders

- Campaign creators
- Donors
- Admins and moderators
